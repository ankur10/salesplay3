User correction: the first prototype felt like generic SaaS; navigation must stay local, every page needs a URL, contacts must be first class, AI needs more prominence, and the top bar can hold search and Ask SalesPlay. Visual reference: /Users/ankurjain/Desktop/mycode/giq2, inspected in its Advisory theme.

## Visual direction
Use the reference's locally served Source Serif 4 / Source Sans 3 typography, editorial reading hierarchy, flat surfaces, fine rules and precise corners. Evolve the palette with a deep navy command bar, cobalt primary actions, ocean teal relationship/AI accents, pale mint AI controls and cool white reading surfaces. The original lavender system is replaced. Do not claim to reproduce giq2 exactly.

## First viewport
A full-width navy command bar makes Ask SalesPlay and account search available everywhere. The white account rail sits below it. The opportunity page pairs the review queue with named contacts in a prominent right column. At phone width, an early people shortcut precedes the queue and the detailed people panel follows it. Primary actions and first rows are readable with real account copy.

## Signature interaction
Search across captured people, opportunities, documents and signals; navigate to the entity's unique local URL; take a selected contact, opportunity, or document into a dedicated AI workspace with visible context. Signals use account-level AI because selected signal context is outside the confirmed assistant capability. URLs support refresh and Back/Forward. AI remains explicitly scripted in this prototype.
