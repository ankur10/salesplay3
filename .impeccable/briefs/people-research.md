Mode: Operate/Read. Local extension of the established SalesPlay system.
User asks for contact upload and opportunity search, detailed opportunities per person, collapsed icon navigation, prominent Accept/Reject, tighter overview spacing, and consistent Signals with unambiguous dates.

OWN-WORLD: Existing fonts, palette picker, flat reading surfaces, local URLs. No backend changes.
FIRST VIEWPORT: People directory with Upload contacts, All/Uploaded views and search. CSV flow: choose file or paste CSV, map fields, review valid/skipped rows, import. Accept/Reject in sticky detail action row. Overview uses available reading space; signals use ordinary rows and dates such as 23 Sept 2026.
STATES: Up to 500 CSV contacts / 2 MB; required name, email validation, duplicate detection, other-account rows skipped, local storage fallback. Imported people do not acquire invented links. FINISH: Browser desktop/mobile captures; import parsing and scope tests; design detector; production build; independent review and scoped docs.
